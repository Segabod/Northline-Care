Why we exist

Some people have always had a doctor on speed dial. Every Canadian should.

Millions of people in Ontario don't have a family doctor, and many who do wait weeks for a short appointment. Walk‑in and virtual queues help in a pinch, but you meet someone new every time and tell your story from the beginning.

Health improves when one clinician knows you, watches what works, and answers when something comes up. Northline Care makes that ongoing relationship possible by message, with phone, video and in person visits when they're needed.

All through messages

How does it work?

Two steps. No waiting room, no phone tag, no booking unless your doctor needs to see you.

1

First, our Care Navigator takes your history.

Describe what's going on in your own words. An AI assistant asks the follow up questions a resident would: when it started, what you've tried, your medications, any warning signs. Add photos or test results if they help.

If you report an emergency symptom, it tells you to call 911 or 988 right away instead of waiting for a reply.

2

Your own doctor replies, usually the same day.

Your chosen doctor reads your original words and the summary, then writes your plan: advice, a prescription sent to your pharmacy, a lab requisition, or a video or in person visit. Most replies arrive the same day.

The AI never diagnoses or prescribes. A licensed clinician signs every plan.

Why the same doctor matters

Why a personal doctor beats a virtual queue.

With your Northline doctor

  • The same doctor reads every message and remembers what you tried last time
  • Your plan is adjusted as results and progress come in
  • Prescriptions, labs and referrals continue without starting over
  • Video or in person visits with the same doctor when needed
  • One record shared with the specialists you add

With a walk‑in virtual queue

  • Whoever is next on shift, a different person each time
  • Your history starts from zero on every call
  • Follow up is up to you, often at another clinic
  • Short, scheduled calls that interrupt your day
  • Notes scattered across services that don't talk to each other
Care that fits your day

Why call, when you can text?

Start a message on the bus, add a photo at lunch, read your doctor's reply after work. No hold music, no booking a day off to sit in a waiting room for a ten minute visit.

When a message isn't enough, your doctor books a phone, video or in person visit at a partner clinic, and it all stays in one conversation.

You pay for unbiased advice, or nothing at all

On Northline Care, the doctor is the care.

Many online health services earn their money on the prescriptions they push. Northline Care doesn't own a pharmacy or sell drugs, so your doctor has no reason to recommend anything except what's right for you.

Care that OHIP covers is billed to OHIP by our partner clinics. You never pay a membership fee to reach your doctor.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Look around inside the prototype →

Is this real healthcare?

Northline Care is designed so that every plan comes from a licensed Ontario clinician working with a partner clinic, under the same standards as an office visit. It is currently a prototype: no clinical services are offered yet, and the doctors shown are fictional.

Which doctors will be on Northline Care?

Family physicians and nurse practitioners first, with specialists such as dermatology, endocrinology, mental health and pediatrics added as the pilot grows. Every clinician's registration is checked with their regulatory college before they can see patients.

Where is it available?

The first launch is planned for Ontario residents with a valid health card. Other provinces will follow once licensing and coverage rules are reviewed for each one. You can join the waitlist for your province today.

How much does it cost?

Care that OHIP covers is free to you, with no membership fee. Services no provincial plan covers, like sick notes and travel consultations, have a clear price shown before you confirm.

How fast will my doctor reply?

Our target is a reply within the same day for most requests, and each doctor's usual response time is shown on their profile. Northline Care isn't for emergencies: call 911, or call or text 988 for a mental health crisis.

What does the AI do, exactly?

The Care Navigator asks follow up questions and drafts a summary for your doctor. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe or reply on your doctor's behalf, and your doctor always sees your original words. You can turn it off in your privacy settings.

How is my information protected?

Health information will be stored in Canada, encrypted, and handled under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act. You can see who has viewed your record and change what you share at any time.
